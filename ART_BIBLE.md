## Core Fantasy Vision
- **Vibe**: Dark fantasy with hope, not pure horror. Massive scale, ancient mythos, high mystery.
- **Visual Benchmark**: Unreal Engine 5 AAA game concept art, cinematic key art, hyper-detailed environments.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Volumetric lighting, dramatic god rays, atmospheric perspective, rich color grading, dynamic clouds, soft fog.

## World Design Guidelines
1. **Architecture**: Grand white and dark stone, ornate gold rune carvings, towering spires, colossal arches, monolithic ruins.
2. **Color Palette**:
   - **Primary Gold**: `#D4AF37` / `#F3C649`
   - **Mystic Blue / Sapphire**: `#5DAEFF` / `#1A3B66`
   - **Deep Shadow / Black**: `#050505` / `#101010`
   - **Crimson & Emerald Accents**: `#E63946` / `#2A9D8F`
3. **Magic & Effects**: Particle embers, ethereal blue fire, ancient glowing runes, void purple rifts, bioluminescent flora.
4. **Character & Race Design Language**: Realistic proportions, worn metal textures, intricate filigree on armor, glowing elemental accents.
